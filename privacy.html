Privacy

Photos, demos, and data handling

GIC Photo Filters either applies an effect on your device or sends your selected photo to the transform API for a one-time result. In the public direct mode, the photo is returned in the response and is not stored by GIC.

Client-side effects

Filters in the Effects & Filters category render locally in the browser. Uploaded pixels stay on-device and the daily free usage budget does not apply.

AI transform intent

For an AI-backed catalog filter, the selected photo is sent to POST /api/transform and processed by the configured AI provider. In the public direct mode, the transformed image is returned immediately in the response; GIC does not persist the source photo, a job record, or the result image.

No account required

The shell does not add sign-in or identity collection. Demo budgeting is expressed as daily limits and neuron counters, not account-based profiles.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

The public app and demo flow do not require an account or key. A separate BYOK builder/deployment flow may be available on the website; its credentials remain in the browser and are not part of the V1 iOS flow.

Operational privacy

Rate limiting and providers

The public direct mode uses anonymous IP-based counters to enforce the free demo allowance. This is abuse prevention, not advertising or cross-site tracking. The AI provider processes the image only to return the requested transform.

  • Direct mode does not persist source or result images.
  • No advertising tracker or analytics profile is used by the V1 app.
  • Deployments that enable R2 storage have different retention behavior and must publish that configuration separately.
