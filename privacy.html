Privacy

Photos, demos, and data handling

The web shell is explicit about what happens to photos: client-side effects never leave the device, AI-backed transforms are intended to flow through backend endpoints later, and the PRD assumes short-lived image retention for server-side processing.

Client-side effects

Filters in the Effects & Filters category render locally in the browser. Uploaded pixels stay on-device and the daily free usage budget does not apply.

AI transform intent

When /api/transform is connected, photos may be processed by AI infrastructure. The PRD targets temporary storage and deletion within 24 hours for those flows.

No account required

The shell does not add sign-in or identity collection. Demo budgeting is expressed as daily limits and neuron counters, not account-based profiles.
