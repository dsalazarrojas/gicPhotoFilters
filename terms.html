Terms

Fair use and launch-ready expectations

These terms describe the live catalog-transform flow: choose a fixed filter, provide a photo you have the right to use, and receive a transformed result. No account is required for the public demo allowance.

Free demo tier

The public service enforces an anonymous IP-based daily demo allowance. Limits may change as the service evolves; do not attempt to bypass them or overload the service.

User content

Only upload photos you have the right to transform and share. Respect likeness, privacy, and copyright considerations when generating results.

Preview mode honesty

Catalog filters use fixed prompts maintained by GIC. The V1 iOS app does not expose the website builder or a free-form prompt editor. Direct-mode transform photos are processed to return a result and are not stored by GIC.

Responsible use

Use the service responsibly

Keep the service safe, lawful, and useful for everyone.

  • Only submit photos you own or are authorized to transform and share.
  • Do not submit unlawful, abusive, or infringing material.
  • Do not attempt to evade rate limits or use the service to build an unauthorized bulk-processing service.
